How I work

  1. 01

    Contact

    You send me your idea. We have a call and go through the details, the main goal and your clients

  2. 02

    Offer

    I send you the price, the deadline and what's included, so everything is clear before we start

  3. 03

    Structure

    We agree on the structure: which pages and sections your website needs and in what order, and I write the texts if needed

  4. 04

    Moodboard

    I show you the style and colors, plus a draft of the site and its first pages, and we approve it together

  5. 05

    Website

    I build the website in the approved style, and it's ready to launch

  6. 06

    Revisions

    If you have edits, we make them: up to three big rounds of revisions are included, then we finish the project
